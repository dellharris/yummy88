The Strategy

Subgenre
Match Is
Everything

The right playlist doesn't just get you streams. It tells the algorithm who you are.

Spotify's recommendation engine — Radio, Autoplay, Discover Weekly — reads which playlists a song lands in and uses that signal to build a listener profile. Broad genre placements muddy that signal. Subgenre placements sharpen it.

We pitch to the playlists that match your song most closely. Not the biggest playlists — the most relevant ones. That match massages the algorithm toward listeners who are already primed for exactly your sound.

Direct from the playbook

"The most important thing is to pitch to the subgenre playlist that matches the song closely. That will massage the Spotify algorithm for most relevant radio suggestions."

Timing

The first 72 hours of a release are when Spotify's algorithm is most actively building a listener map for the track. Playlist adds that happen after the release window carry less weight.

We coordinate with curators ahead of your release date to ensure placements go live on day one — or as close to it as possible. That timing multiplies the value of every placement.

Why it matters
Day 1 adds = peak algorithm impact
Subgenre match = relevant radio
One sheet = curator confidence

Every pitch goes out with a one sheet — artist context, track details, genre and subgenre framing. Curators make faster yes decisions when the story is clear.

Release
Day or
Bust

Placements that come in late can still move the needle, but they miss the first-week window where Spotify's systems are most actively learning about the track. We move on the release schedule, not around it.

The Process

How It
Works

Five steps from submission to placement. We handle everything after you submit your track.

Step 01
One Sheet
We build your one sheet — artist bio, track context, subgenre framing, release date, and any supporting social proof. This goes to every curator and is the difference between a real consideration and a skip.
Step 02
Subgenre Map
We identify the subgenre playlists that match your track most precisely. Not by follower count — by sound match. A 5K playlist in exactly your subgenre is worth more than a 100K catch-all.
Step 03
Curator Pitch
Direct outreach to our trusted curator network. These aren't cold pitches to a marketplace — these are relationships. We pitch with your one sheet and coordinate timing from the start.
Step 04
Day One Timing
We coordinate with confirmed curators to align adds with your release date. The goal is always day one. When that's not possible, we get as close as the curator's schedule allows.
Step 05
Algorithm Unlock
Subgenre placements on day one feed Spotify the strongest possible listener signal. The algorithm uses that signal to build your radio profile — putting your music in front of people most likely to stream, save, and follow.
The Goal
Relevant Radio
Spotify Radio built from accurate subgenre data = listeners who actually fit your sound. That's the compounding effect. It doesn't just get you streams — it builds the right audience profile that keeps generating them.
